Privacy Policy

Postroom is a private scheduling tool operated by its owner for their own social media accounts.

What we store

When you connect an Instagram, YouTube or TikTok account, Postroom stores the access tokens the platform issues (encrypted), basic profile details (username, display name, profile picture, follower count), the media you upload, the posts you schedule, and post statistics (views, likes, comments, shares). We never receive or store your passwords.

How it is used

Only to publish the posts you schedule and to show you how they perform. Data is never sold or shared with third parties, apart from the platform you publish to and our hosting providers (Vercel, Supabase).

Deleting your data

Disconnect the account in Postroom (Accounts → Disconnect), or remove Postroom from the platform’s connected-apps settings. Tokens are deleted immediately; scheduled posts for that account are cancelled. To have all remaining data deleted, contact the operator of this Postroom instance.
